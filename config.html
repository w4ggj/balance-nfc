Video background YouTube on the main TV

Plays in the main panel where standings/leaderboards show — the weekly-events column and bottom ticker stay up. Great for fail reels or trailers between events.

Two kinds of link work:
• A direct video file (a link ending in .mp4 / .webm) — recommended for the TV, it plays reliably on the Fire Stick. Upload a clip to Shopify Files and paste its link.
• A YouTube video, playlist, /live/… or /shorts/… link.

Hear sound but the video area is white on the Fire Stick? That's the Fire Stick's decoder, not the link — newer videos and lives are served in a format it can't paint, older ones aren't. Add &vq=medium to the TV's start URL to ask YouTube for a format it can (see KIOSK_SETUP.md), or use an .mp4.

Also: some channels block playback outside YouTube entirely — those play no sound and the TV reports the reason right here.
